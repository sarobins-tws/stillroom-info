The Whisky Stillroom — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026.

Operator: Scott Robinson. Contact: whiskystillroom@gmail.com.

Your collection and journal

Your preferred name, whisky and bottle details, tasting notes and scores, blind-session history, imported collection information, and photos are stored in the app on your device. The developer does not receive this personal collection or journal through the app, except for the product details you explicitly submit as a catalog suggestion. When iCloud is available, Stillroom automatically syncs this information through your private iCloud database using the iCloud account in device Settings. Apple provides that storage and sync service. Your private collection and journal are not accessible through the developer’s public catalog or review sheet.

You control the information you enter and the photos you choose. The system photo picker gives the app access to the photos you select. Camera access is requested when you take a photo. Optional background blur processes the photo on your device.

You can export your collection and tasting text as CSV files and choose where to save or share them using the system share sheet. These files exclude photos and recently deleted recovery copies. The app does not automatically send exports to the developer. Copies you save or share are managed separately from the app’s stored data.

Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is required to use Stillroom. Apple handles sign-in. The app stores your Apple-provided app identifier securely on the device so you can reopen it. If Apple supplies a name, the app can suggest it when you confirm your preferred name. The app does not request your email address. It does not operate a developer-hosted account database. Signing out removes the local sign-in session and keeps your collection and journal.

Catalog updates

The app includes an offline whisky catalog. While signed in and connected to the internet, it checks Apple's CloudKit public database when the app launches, when it receives a silent catalog-release push, or when you manually refresh. It does not check merely because the app returns to the foreground, and it does not poll on a timer. The silent push tells the app to check for a new catalog; it contains no personal collection or journal information. Updates download without requiring you to review matching or conflicting whisky details immediately. Your ordinary catalog searches stay on your device. Your collection, tasting notes, and photos are not uploaded to the public catalog. Apple operates the CloudKit service under its own privacy policies.

Catalog suggestions

Catalog suggestions are optional. When you check “Suggest this whisky for the Stillroom catalog” before saving a manual whisky or its edits, the app sends only that whisky’s product details for developer review. Purchase information, bottle-specific details, private notes, tasting notes and scores, photos, and your preferred name are excluded. Unchecked entries and CSV imports are not automatically submitted.

Suggestions are queued locally while offline or iCloud is unavailable. They are sent to a separate submission inbox using Apple CloudKit and the iCloud account on your device. CloudKit associates records with its account identifier; the app does not include your Apple sign-in identifier or email in the submitted product details. The developer may copy submissions into a private Google Sheet in the Stillroom Google Drive account to verify and clean them. Accepted product information may become part of the official public catalog. Submission does not guarantee acceptance. For questions or removal requests regarding a submitted suggestion, contact whiskystillroom@gmail.com.

Tracking and advertising

The app includes no advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics SDK, or developer-run telemetry service. It does not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple's TestFlight service handles beta installation and may make testing and crash information available to the developer. If you send beta feedback, the developer receives the information and screenshots you choose to share through TestFlight. Feedback is used to investigate problems and improve the app. Please avoid including personal details you do not want to share. TestFlight and Apple services are also governed by Apple's privacy policies.

Managing your information

You can edit your saved details and remove bottles or tastings in the app. Deleting a bottle can preserve its historical information in associated tastings, so remove the relevant journal entries as well if you want that history removed. Deleted bottles and completed tastings have separate recovery copies in More → Recently Deleted for 30 days. You can restore them or delete those copies permanently earlier. Expired copies are removed when you next use the app. Discarded unfinished tastings are not kept there. Local recovery backups made during app upgrades can contain older data separately from Recently Deleted. Removing the app removes its local app data and those local upgrade backups from that device. Removing the app or signing out does not delete data already stored in iCloud; backups made by iOS or other device-backup services are controlled separately in those services. Signing out is not a data-deletion action.

Stillroom Catalog Tools — developer's Google connection

Stillroom Catalog Tools is a separate Mac utility used by the developer to maintain the catalog. Ordinary Stillroom app users do not sign into Google or authorize this utility.

The utility uses Google sign-in to check the authorized email address and requests spreadsheet editing and Drive file-metadata reading. It checks that the configured review spreadsheet belongs to the dedicated Stillroom Google account. It reads and updates the configured spreadsheet's review queue, original submitted product details and catalog master. Drive metadata is used to check ownership; the utility does not request Gmail, contacts, photos or general Drive file-content access.

Google credentials are stored with owner-only permissions on the authorized Mac, outside the app and its source repository. They are used for the catalog review and publication workflow, and are not included in the distributed app. The review spreadsheet is private to the Stillroom account. Only vetted whisky product information is published in the official catalog.

The developer can revoke this utility's access through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Google account access and Google Drive storage are also governed by Google's privacy policies. Questions about this utility can be sent to whiskystillroom@gmail.com.
