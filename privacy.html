Your collection and journal

Your preferred name, whisky and bottle details, tasting notes and scores, blind-session history, imported collection information, and photos are stored in the app on your device. The developer does not receive this personal collection or journal through the app, except for the product details you explicitly submit as a catalog suggestion. When iCloud is available, the app syncs personal records and photos through your private Apple CloudKit database. Your private database is separate from the public catalog and catalog-suggestion inbox. Sync uses the iCloud account on the device.

You control the information you enter and the photos you choose. The system photo picker gives the app access to the photos you select. Camera access is requested when you take a photo. Optional background blur processes the photo on your device.

You can export your collection and tasting text as CSV files and choose where to save or share them using the system share sheet. These files exclude photos and recently deleted recovery copies. The app does not send exports to the developer. Copies you save or share are managed separately from the app’s stored data.

Sign in with Apple

Apple handles sign-in. The app stores your Apple-provided app identifier securely on the device so you can reopen it. If Apple supplies a name, the app can suggest it when you confirm your preferred name. The app does not request your email or operate a developer-hosted account database. Signing out removes the local sign-in session and keeps your collection and journal.

Catalog updates

The app includes an offline whisky catalog. While signed in and connected to the internet, it checks Apple's CloudKit public database when the app launches, when it receives a silent catalog-release push, or when you manually refresh. Catalog searches stay on your device. Your collection, tasting notes, and photos are not uploaded to the public catalog. Apple operates the CloudKit service under its own privacy policies.

Catalog suggestions

Catalog suggestions are optional. When you check “Suggest this whisky for the Stillroom catalog” before saving a manual whisky or its edits, the app sends only that whisky’s product details for developer review. Purchase information, bottle-specific details, private notes, tasting notes and scores, photos, and your preferred name are excluded. Unchecked entries and CSV imports are not submitted.

Suggestions are submitted to Apple CloudKit, which delivers them to a separate review inbox and associates pending submissions with your iCloud account identifier. Your Apple sign-in identifier and email are not included in the product details.

Submitted whisky product facts are transferred to a private Google spreadsheet for catalog review, without your name, email or submission identifiers. Please leave personal and contact details out of product descriptions. The account-linked CloudKit submission is deleted after that transfer is confirmed. Pending submissions remain in CloudKit until transfer or deletion; Settings → Delete Account & All Data removes your pending submissions when you are online. Product facts already transferred without a link to you may remain for review or inclusion in the catalog. Submission does not guarantee acceptance. For questions, contact whiskystillroom@gmail.com.

Tracking and advertising

The app includes no advertising, cross-app tracking, analytics SDK, or developer-run telemetry service. It does not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

TestFlight and feedback

Apple's TestFlight service handles beta installation and may make testing and crash information available to the developer. If you send beta feedback, the developer receives the information and screenshots you choose to share through TestFlight. Feedback is used to investigate problems and improve the app. Please avoid including personal details you do not want to share. TestFlight and Apple services are also governed by Apple's privacy policies.

Managing your information

You can edit your saved details and remove bottles or tastings in the app. Deleting a bottle can preserve its historical information in associated tastings, so remove the relevant journal entries as well if you want that history removed. Deleted bottles and completed tastings can be recovered from More -> Recently Deleted for 30 days, or deleted permanently. Expired copies are removed when you next use the app. Local recovery backups made during app upgrades can contain older data separately from Recently Deleted. Removing the app removes its local app data and those local upgrade backups from that device; backups made by iOS or other device-backup services are controlled separately in those services. Signing out is not a data-deletion action.

Settings → Delete Account & All Data permanently clears personal records, photos, Recently Deleted items, local upgrade recovery copies, temporary app exports, and the saved Apple sign-in identity. Private cloud deletion requires the matching iCloud account and a connection. An unfinished request remains on the device and can be retried after reopening the app; the app does not report completion while cloud removal is pending. A minimal random deletion receipt and dates remain in the private database so updated devices can recognize that an old local collection must be removed. The receipt contains no collection, name, note, or photo.

Exports saved outside the app and backups managed by Apple or other backup services are controlled separately. To remove the app's Apple sign-in authorization, use device Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Stillroom → Delete.

Privacy requests

Contact whiskystillroom@gmail.com to ask about information handled by the developer, request access, correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. We normally respond within one calendar month. Identity verification will be proportionate to the request. Your device’s private collection is not accessible through the developer’s catalog tools; we can help you use the app’s controls to manage it. The CSV export is a convenience and excludes photos and recovery copies. It is not a complete copy of every item for a privacy access request. If applicable law provides these rights, you may also request portability, restriction or object to processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. Removing identifying pending submissions does not remove product facts already kept without a link to you.
