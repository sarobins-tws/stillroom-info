The Whisky Stillroom

A personal whisky collection and tasting journal for iPhone and iPad.

Keep track of your bottles, record tastings, and explore an evolving whisky catalog. You can optionally suggest a manually entered whisky for the Stillroom catalog. Suggestions include product details for review; purchase details, private notes, tasting scores and photos are excluded.

Sign in with Apple gives you access to Stillroom. Your collection and journal are stored on your device and sync through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Catalog updates download behind the scenes, with optional review of changes to saved whiskies.

Stillroom Catalog Tools lets the developer review submitted whisky product facts and maintain the catalog in a private Google spreadsheet. App users do not need to sign into Google; personal collections, tasting notes and photos are not transferred to that spreadsheet.
