The Whisky Stillroom

A personal whisky collection and tasting journal for iPhone and iPad.

Keep track of your bottles, record tastings, and explore an evolving whisky catalog. You can optionally suggest a manually entered whisky for the Stillroom catalog. Suggestions include product details for review; purchase details, private notes, tasting scores and photos are excluded.

The current beta stores your personal collection and journal on your device. It does not yet sync that information between devices.

Stillroom Catalog Tools is a separate Mac utility used by the developer to review suggestions and maintain the official catalog through a private Google spreadsheet. Ordinary app users do not need a Google account or access to the developer's tools.
